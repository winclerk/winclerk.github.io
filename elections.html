Getting registered & voting
New in town? Voting by mail? Start here.

Just moved to Winchester? Give the Clerk's office a call and we'll help you register — it only takes a minute. Prefer to vote from home, planning to be out of town, or unable to make it on election day? You can request an absentee ballot anytime at MyVote Wisconsin. First time requesting one online? You'll just need to upload a photo of your ID with the request.
