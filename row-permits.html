Permit Requirements

Wisconsin law requires a permit before any person excavates, fills, installs a culvert, or otherwise alters a public road — issued by the authority that maintains that road Wis. Stat. § 86.07(2)(a). Utility lines, wires, fiber, and pipes placed along, across, under, or within a road right-of-way require the written consent of that same authority § 86.16(1).

For roads maintained by the Town of Winchester, the Town is that authority. This page covers two categories of permits:

  • Right-of-way and utility permits — for any work by a utility company, contractor, or property owner within the Town road right-of-way, including buried and overhead utility installation, road crossings, directional bores, ditch and drainage work, vegetation removal, and temporary use of the right-of-way.
  • Driveway permits — for the establishment, construction, reconstruction, rerouting, or alteration of a driveway providing access to a Town road, governed by Ordinance 2024-04.
Jurisdiction. The Town's permit authority extends only to roads it maintains. County Trunk Highways W, J, and O pass through Winchester but are maintained by Vilas County. Work on those roads requires a permit from the County, not the Town. See the jurisdiction table below.

Right-of-Way & Utility Permits

Scope

A right-of-way permit is required for any of the following work within a Town-maintained road, easement, or public property:

  • Installation of buried utilities — electric, fiber optic, natural gas, water, or communications — by mainline extension, service lateral, or road crossing
  • Installation or modification of overhead utilities — poles, guys, anchors, and spans
  • Directional boring or jack-and-bore operations beneath the road
  • Excavation, grading, or drainage work within the right-of-way
  • Vegetation or tree removal within the right-of-way
  • Temporary use of the right-of-way for staging, equipment, or materials
  • Any other excavation, fill, or alteration of a Town road not covered by a driveway permit

Application Process

Contact the Town Clerk at 715-686-2123 or clerk@winchester.wi.gov to initiate a right-of-way permit application. The Clerk will provide the application form and identify whether the work requires Town Board approval at a regular meeting or can be processed administratively.

Applicants should be prepared to provide:

  • A site plan or sketch showing the location and extent of proposed work relative to the road centerline and right-of-way boundaries
  • Installation method, depth of cover, and materials
  • Traffic control plan if the work will affect the travelled way
  • Certificate of insurance naming the Town of Winchester as additional insured
  • Diggers Hotline ticket number (may be filed after the permit is issued but before work begins)
  • Projected start and completion dates
  • Name and phone number of the on-site contact for public notification
60-day statutory deadline. For utility company applications, state law requires the Town to approve or deny within 60 days of receipt. If no action is taken, the permit is deemed granted by operation of law § 182.017(9). A denial must be accompanied by written reasons provided to the applicant at the time of denial.
Driveway Permits

Ordinance 2024-04 — Driveway Ordinance

No person may establish, construct, reconstruct, reroute, or alter the slope of a driveway without first obtaining a permit from the Town Board § V(C). The applicant must own or have legal interest in the property to which the permit will apply § V(D).

Private Roads Serving Three or More Residences

Private roads providing access to more than three residences are governed by the Town of Winchester Road Ordinance (Ordinance 2023-02) rather than the Driveway Ordinance. Requirements include a 66 ft easement, 12 ft minimum graveled width over 4 in. compacted gravel, a 50 ft diameter turnaround, bridges rated for a 30-ton fire apparatus at 10 ft minimum width, and 15 ft overhead clearance Ord. 2024-04 § VI(F). Contact the Clerk for the full Road Ordinance requirements.

Application and Approval

1

Submit the application

A completed application, the non-refundable $100 fee, and a sketch map showing property lines, grade, slope, width, length, turnoffs, and erosion control. The proposed driveway must be staked out prior to inspection. Submit to the Town Clerk. § VII(A), (B)

2

Board review

The Town Board reviews applications at its regular meeting — the second Monday of the month at 6:00 PM, Town Hall, 7228 CTH W. The Board may require additional documentation including plat maps, aerial photos, soil or slope analysis, and construction plans § VII(B)(2). Submit these with the initial application where possible to avoid delays.

3

Board decision

The Board will approve, deny, or conditionally approve the permit. Conditions attached to a permit are binding § VII(C). A denial will include a written explanation of the facts on which it was based § VII(D). After two consecutive denials on the same parcel, a subsequent application may not be considered for 60 days § VII(D)(2).

4

Construction and completion

Call Diggers Hotline (811) at least three working days before excavation. Upon completion, notify the Town Board within 30 days. The Board will inspect within 30 days of notification to confirm compliance with the permit and the ordinance § VII(E).

Do not order culvert material before the permit is issued. The minimum specification is 12 in. × 24 ft, but the Board may require a larger diameter based on site conditions and ditch grade.

Emergency Work

Where an emergency requires immediate work to protect public safety or restore service — such as a utility outage, gas leak, water main break, or road washout — the work may proceed without advance approval. The responsible party must notify the Town Clerk at 715-686-2123 or the Town road crew at 715-543-2385 by the end of the next business day and submit the permit application as an after-the-fact filing. All restoration and inspection requirements remain in effect.
