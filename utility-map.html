Coverage is reported, not guaranteed. Maps show what providers report to the FCC and the Wisconsin PSC. Call the provider to confirm service at your address before you buy or build.

Cell coverage is the FCC’s outdoor, stationary standard: 4G LTE at 5/1 Mbps and 5G at 7/1 Mbps. Indoor and in-car service can be weaker.

Fiber is shown by FCC hexagon (about 0.3 sq mi), not by individual address. Wireline data does not include MVNOs or satellite.

Sources: FCC National Broadband Map (data as of Dec 31, 2025); PSC of Wisconsin electric service territories (approximate); U.S. Census town boundary. Think a provider’s coverage is wrong? You can file a challenge on the FCC map.
